Yes. I checked the Waters Empower 3 Data Acquisition and Processing Theory Guide, and there is an important correction to my earlier wording:

Amount is a built-in Empower peak/result field, not a user-defined custom field.

For the common case where the calibration curve X-value is Amount, the core calculation is:

$$ \boxed{ Amount = X_{\text{from calibration curve}} \times \frac{Dilution}{SampleWeight} } $$

where Empower determines \(X\) by applying the unknown peak's response \(Y\) to the calibration curve. This sequence is explicitly described in the Waters Empower 3 Data Acquisition and Processing Theory Guide.

For a linear calibration

If your calibration curve is:

$$ Y = B X + A $$

then:

$$ X = \frac{Y-A}{B} $$

Therefore:

$$ \boxed{ Amount = \frac{Y-A}{B} \times \frac{Dilution}{SampleWeight} } $$

Where:

\(Y\) = sample response — usually Area, but it can be Height or another configured Y-value
\(A\) = calibration curve intercept
\(B\) = calibration curve slope
Dilution = sample Dilution field
SampleWeight = sample SampleWeight field

Waters confirms that during quantitation Empower divides the calibration-derived X-value by SampleWeight and multiplies it by Dilution. Waters WKB96394

For example:

$$ Y=10500,\quad A=500,\quad B=1000 $$

So:

$$ X=(10500-500)/1000=10 $$

If:

SampleWeight = 0.5
Dilution = 10

then:

$$ Amount=10/0.5\times10=\boxed{200} $$

There is another very important layer: if X-Value = Concentration instead of Amount, Empower obtains Concentration from the curve and derives Amount using Injection Volume. Waters documents that relationship too.

So the complete built-in Amount logic actually depends on X-Value, Y-Value, calibration fit, SampleWeight, Dilution, and potentially Injection Volume.

For a single-standard, single-sample assay using peak Area:

$$ \boxed{ Assay(\%)= \frac{SampleArea}{StandardArea} \times \frac{StandardWeight}{SampleWeight} \times \frac{SampleDilution}{StandardDilution} \times StandardPotency(\%) } $$

(100-LOD_Spl-Water_Spl-OVI_Spl) = sample’s corrected as-is basis.
MW1/MW2 = molecular-weight conversion, such as salt-to-active or active-to-salt correction.
Label_Claim = target amount per dosage unit or declared strength.
Final *100 = converts the label-claim ratio into assay percentage.

1/SD1*SD2/SD3*SD4/SD5*SD6/SD7*TD1/1*TD3/TD2

Presented with grouping:

(1/SD1)*(SD2/SD3)*(SD4/SD5)*(SD6/SD7)*
(TD1/1)*(TD3/TD2)
